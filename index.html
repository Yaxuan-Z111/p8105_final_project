Multivariable Logistic Analysis of Type II Diabetes Risk Considering Demographic, Mental Health and Lifestyles

Overview

This project investigates whether anxiety, depression, and smoking are significant predictors of T2D incidence, after accounting for key demographic and clinical covariates. The goal is to quantify their independent and joint associations with T2D risk and to assess whether these factors should be considered in risk stratification and prevention strategies.

The link to the repository of this project can be found here.

Type II Diabetes

Type 2 diabetes (T2D) is a major global public health problem. In the United States alone, more than 38 million people are living with diabetes, and an estimated 90%–95% of these cases are type 2 diabetes. As a chronic condition, T2D is associated with substantial morbidity and an average reduction in life expectancy of around ten years. At the same time, T2D can often be prevented or delayed through evidence-based lifestyle changes, including weight management, healthy eating, and regular physical activity. (CDC, 2024)

Data Source

We use data from the 2024 National Health Interview Survey (NHIS), a nationally representative, cross-sectional survey of the civilian noninstitutionalized U.S. population conducted by the National Center for Health Statistics (NCHS). T2D status, anxiety, depression, smoking, and relevant demographic and clinical covariates are derived from the publicly available NHIS sample adult-level files.

Learn more about the data here.

Statistical Analysis

Accounting for the complex survey design using appropriate sampling weights and design variables, we fit generalized linear models (GLMs) with T2D status as the binary outcome and include all selected NHIS variables as candidate predictors in the same model. We then extend these models with interaction terms and use nested model comparisons to assess effect modification and potential confounding.

T2D Prevalence Visualization

The New York State T2D Map

df_raw = read_csv(
  "DiabetesAtlas_CountyData.csv",
  skip = 2
)

ny_df = df_raw %>%
  filter(State == "New York") %>%
  mutate(
    CountyFIPS = sprintf("%05d", as.numeric(CountyFIPS)),
    Percentage = as.numeric(Percentage),
    hover = paste0(
      "County: ", County, 
      "<br>Percentage: ", Percentage, "%"
    )
  )

geo_url = "https://raw.githubusercontent.com/plotly/datasets/master/geojson-counties-fips.json"

fig = plot_ly(
  data = ny_df,
  type = "choropleth",
  geojson = geo_url,
  locations = ~CountyFIPS,
  z = ~Percentage,
  colorscale = "Blues",
  text = ~hover,
  hoverinfo = "text",
  marker = list(line = list(width = 0))
) %>%
  layout(
    title = "Prevalence of T2D by County (New York State)",
    geo = list(
      fitbounds = "locations",
      visible = FALSE
    )
  )
